Two judgements, kept apart. Interpretation belongs to the recording and does not change: Pinnock in 1982 played what he played. Sound belongs to the edition — the pressing, the transfer, the remastering — and changes every time a label reissues it. Stars and Référence describe the interpretation only. The sound figure describes one specific disc, which is the thing you are actually holding in a shop.

How a claim earns its weight. Source class sets a base weight; independence and citation scale it. A cited statement counts fully, a named source without a locator counts about seven-tenths, an uncited draft about a third. Commercial interest in the recording cuts the weight outright. This is what lets the catalogue accept contributions from readers and from research agents without letting either move a score before someone has checked the receipt.
